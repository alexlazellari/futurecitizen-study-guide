# Chapter 4: A Modern, Thriving Society

"A Modern, Thriving Society" is the fourth chapter of the official Life in the UK
handbook. It covers everyday British culture — how people live, what they
celebrate, what they watch and play, and the places they visit.

Most people find this chapter more enjoyable to read than the history chapter,
but it is still dense with specific facts. Expect questions on named people,
dates, festivals and places rather than general themes.

## What this chapter covers

1. **[The UK today](/ch4/the-uk-today)** — population, languages and the make-up
   of modern British society
2. **[Religion](/ch4/religion)** — the main faiths practised in the UK, and the
   Church of England's role
3. **[Customs and traditions](/ch4/customs-and-traditions)** — the main
   festivals and holidays across the year
4. **[Sport](/ch4/sport)** — the sports Britain is known for and the people
   associated with them
5. **[Arts and culture](/ch4/arts-and-culture)** — music, theatre, literature,
   architecture and film
6. **[Leisure](/ch4/leisure)** — how people spend their free time
7. **[Places of interest](/ch4/places-of-interest)** — landmarks and
   destinations across the four nations

<Callout type="info" emoji="💡">
  **Tip:** This chapter rewards learning names and dates. When you read about a
  composer, athlete or festival, note the name and what it is known for — that
  is usually the level of detail the test asks for.
</Callout>
